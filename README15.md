The brute-force approach compares every ID with every other ID.
Its time complexity is O(n²), making it impractical for large datasets.
The set-based approach uses hashing for constant-time lookups.
Its time complexity is O(n), making it suitable for real-world systems.
For datasets containing millions of records, the set-based approach performs significantly better.
